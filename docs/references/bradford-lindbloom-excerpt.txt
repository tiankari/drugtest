Chromatic adaptation: Bradford cone-response matrix and reference whites
EXCERPT for scripts/build-kit-profile.ts (Munsell data, illuminant C -> D65).

Source:   Bruce Lindbloom, "Chromatic Adaptation", http://www.brucelindbloom.com/Eqn_ChromAdapt.html
          (page http://www.brucelindbloom.com/index.html?Eqn_ChromAdapt.html)
Fetched:  2026-09-28
Method:   numbers copied from the page's tables; the adaptation is
            [M] = inverse(M_A) * diag(rho_D/rho_S, gamma_D/gamma_S, beta_D/beta_S) * M_A
          with (rho, gamma, beta) = M_A * (reference white XYZ), as stated on the page.

Bradford M_A (row-major):
   0.8951000   0.2664000  -0.1614000
  -0.7502000   1.7135000   0.0367000
   0.0389000  -0.0685000   1.0296000

Bradford inverse(M_A) as printed on the page (the script inverts M_A itself and checks it against this):
   0.9869929  -0.1470543   0.1599627
   0.4323053   0.5183603   0.0492912
  -0.0085287   0.0400428   0.9684867

Reference white tristimulus values from the page (it states they come from ASTM E308-01):
  Illuminant C    X 0.98074   Y 1.00000   Z 1.18232
  Illuminant D65  X 0.95047   Y 1.00000   Z 1.08883

Destination white used by the app: NOT the D65 row above but the D65 white of
src/pipeline/colour.ts (from chromaticity x 0.3127, y 0.3290: X 0.950456, Z 1.089058),
so that paper white adapts exactly to the white of the app's CIELAB. The two D65 rows
differ by 0.00001 in X and 0.00023 in Z.
