## Plain limits

- **POSITIVE has not been tested on a real reaction.** It appears above only on rendered images. No stand-in object may be used to fake a POSITIVE in a demo.
- The target colours are Munsell notations published by NIJ in 2000 (the colour of each reaction as seen against Munsell chips), converted to CIELAB. They were never measured through this app, this card or these phones.
- **The absolute accuracy of our correction is unknown**, because the card is calibrated against our own print (registered on one phone at dusk), not against a measured colour standard. The correction-error term in the radius is the leave-one-out error on 3 real photos of one object on one phone.
- NEGATIVE means only that no colour developed in the sample zone. The app cannot tell that from an empty zone; the officer must confirm the test is in the zone, and the signed photo shows which it was.
- A real deployment needs NCB’s forensic laboratory to photograph real Marquis reactions (each drug, several amounts, several phones and lights) on the registered card with this app, and to replace these numbers with the measured spread (then `validation` becomes `validated-on-real-photos`).
