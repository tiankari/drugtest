## 1. Test colour in the sample zone across lightings

The test colour is read from the central 24 × 24 mm of the sample zone (trimmed median, 10% cut at each end of the luma order). "Before" is the camera's own colour, converted to CIELAB (D65) without correction; "after" is flat-fielded and corrected to the registered reference (paper white = L* 100). Spread across lightings = the largest CIEDE2000 between any two photos in the group: how differently the same colour reads under different lights. Smaller is better.

| highlightClipLevel | 254 | provisional | Browser YUV-to-RGB conversion can land saturated pixels on 254 as well as 255. |
| maxHighlightClipFraction | 0.02 | provisional | Blown paper white destroys the white reference; 2% allows a few specular pixels. Saturated printed colours can clip one channel legitimately, so that is left to the per-patch check. |
| shadowClipLevel | 3 | provisional | Printed black markers normally photograph well above 3; only true underexposure crushes to 0-3. |
| maxShadowClipFraction | 0.1 | provisional | Corner markers are under 4% of the card area, so more than 10% crushed means the frame is too dark. |
| minMedianLuma | 50 | provisional | The card is mostly white; a median this low means the scene is far too dark for colour work. |
| idCellMargin | 0.15 | provisional | Printed cells read near marker black or paper white; one within 15% of the midpoint is not clearly either, so the ID is refused rather than guessed. |
| minPatchSourcePixels | 400 | provisional | About 4 px/mm: below that, lens blur and a 1 mm placement error eat into the 5 mm square and the median mixes in the neighbouring colours. |
| maxClipFraction | 0.02 | provisional | A clipped channel is not a measurement; 2% allows stray specks but not a glare spot. |
| maxResidualWhiteRatio | 1.2 | provisional | A smooth gradient is now corrected (user decision, 2026-09-28); what remains is local shadow the plane cannot model. Real registration photos leave 1.03-1.06, the cap-shadow warm-bulb photo 2.26. To be derived from the retake set. |
