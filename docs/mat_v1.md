# MAT v1 — the reference colour card

Source of truth: `src/pipeline/mat.ts`. Printable files are generated by
`npm run mat` into `print/` (see `print/PRINT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`). A machine-
readable copy of this layout is in `print/mat_v1_layout.json`.

## Size and fit

A6 portrait, 105 × 148 mm. Everything required fits **without shrinking any
patch below 10 mm**:

| Element | Size / position |
|---|---|
| Corner markers | 4 × solid black 12 mm squares, 4 mm from the trim |
| Orientation key | 4 mm white square in the centre of the **top-left** marker only |
| Colour patches | 30 × 10 mm squares in a ring: 6 across the top, 6 across the bottom, 9 down each side |
| Quiet zone | ≥ 2.5 mm white between every marker and any patch (tested) |
| ID strip | 9 cells × 5 mm, centred, 19 mm from the top |
| Labels | "MAT v1" and "COPY <letter>" under the ID strip |
| Sample zone | 70 × 70 mm, outlined, plain white, x 17.5–87.5, y 38–108 (centre 4 mm above the card centre) |
| Scale bar | 50 mm, five alternating 10 mm segments, under the sample zone |

The 30 ring cells are: a **6-step neutral ramp** (white to near-black), **5
extra white patches**, and **19 chromatic patches** (16 reaction colours plus
primary red, green and blue). The extra whites are W_T (top row), W_B (bottom
row), W_L (left column, lower) and W_R1/W_R2 (right column, upper and lower),
so every side has one. With the ramp's own white N1 at the top of the left
column, there is a white patch near each of the four corners plus the middle
of the top and bottom edges: six points for the uneven-light check.

## Orientation

The four markers sit at the corners of an 85 × 128 mm rectangle (centre to
centre). Only the top-left marker has a white centre. Given the four marker
centres in a photo, the holed marker fixes which corner is top-left; the rear
camera does not mirror, so going clockwise from it gives TR, BR, BL. That
resolves 90°, 180° and 270° rotations unambiguously. The rectangle's aspect
(short side TL→TR, long side TL→BL) is a second, independent check that also
catches a mirrored image.

## ID strip

Nine black/white cells read left to right on the upright card:

- cells 0–3: mat version (1–14), most significant bit first
- cells 4–7: copy index, A = 1 … N = 14
- cell 8: parity, set so the number of **black** cells is **odd**

Values 0 and 15 are never issued, so an all-white strip (washed out, missing)
and an all-black strip (shadowed, covered) both fail to decode. Every
single-cell misread fails parity (tested for all copies). An unreadable strip
or failed parity means RETAKE.

| Copy | Cells (1 = black) |
|---|---|
| A | `000100011` |
| B | `000100101` |

Neutral ramp values are L* = 100, 82, 65, 49, 34, 20 converted to sRGB and
frozen. Chromatic design colours were chosen to sit inside a typical CMYK
printer gamut (moderate chroma) while covering the families field reactions
produce: yellows (turmeric/curcumin yellow included), oranges, reddish-browns
(the alkaline turmeric colour), browns, purples, violets, blues and
blue-greens.
