# Printing the reference colour card (MAT v1)

The app corrects colours by comparing the photo against this card. If the card
is printed wrongly, every reading made with it is wrong. Take five minutes to
get it right.

## What to print

Print **`mat_A4_sheet.pdf`**. It holds two copies of the card, **A** and **B**,
on one A4 page, with crop marks. Each copy has its own ID strip, so the app can
tell which physical card is in every photo.

(`mat_v1_A.pdf` and `mat_v1_B.pdf` are single A6 cards. Only use them if you
know your printer prints A6 at exactly 100%. Many printers quietly shrink A6.)

## Printer settings

1. **Colour.** Black-and-white printing ruins the card. Check the preview shows
   the coloured squares.
2. **Paper size: A4.**
3. **Scale: "Actual size" or "100%".** Turn **off** "Fit to page", "Shrink
   oversized pages", "Scale to fit" or anything similar.
4. **Paper: matte card, about 250–300 gsm.** Not glossy photo paper: gloss
   causes glare. Plain office paper works for a trial, but it is thin and can
   show through.
5. Use the printer's normal or best quality. Do not use "draft" or "eco" mode.

## After printing

1. **Check the size.** Put a ruler on the **50 mm scale bar** under the sample
   zone. It must measure 50 mm. If it measures anything else (for example
   47 mm), the printer rescaled the page: fix the settings and print again.
2. **Do not laminate** and do not put the card in a plastic sleeve. Plastic
   reflects light and causes glare on the colour squares.
3. **Cut on the crop marks** (the short black lines at the corners of each
   card). Keep the cut straight; a little white margin left over is fine.
4. **Let the ink dry** for an hour before photographing it.

## Before using a card

- **Register every copy in the app** (three photos in soft daylight) before
  using it for tests. The app compares every later photo against these
  registered photos, not against the colours in the file.
- **Never swap in a new print without registering it.** Two prints of the same
  file never come out exactly the same colour, even on the same printer. A
  reprinted card must get a new copy letter and be registered as a new card.
- Keep the card clean, dry and flat, out of direct sunlight when not in use.
  If it gets stained, faded, bent or wet, stop using it and register a new one.

## What is on the card

- Four black corner squares. The one with a white square in its middle marks
  the top-left corner, so the app always knows which way up the card is.
- A strip of nine black-and-white cells under the top row: it encodes the card
  version (MAT v1) and the copy letter.
- 30 colour squares around the edge: a grey ramp from white to near-black,
  white squares on every side (for checking the light is even), and the colours
  field test reactions produce.
- The outlined **sample zone** in the middle, where the test tube, strip or
  small glass stands.
- The 50 mm scale bar and the label "MAT v1".
